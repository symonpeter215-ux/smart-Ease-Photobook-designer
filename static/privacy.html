Privacy Policy

Smart Ease · Ease Creative Suite (Smart Ease Photobook Studio, Ease Select, Ease Retouch Academy) · Last updated 28 October 2026

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. If you create a password, we store only a secure one-way hash of it, never the password itself.
  • Google sign-in: if you choose "Continue with Google", Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We do not get your Google password.
  • Plan and payment records: which plan you have, when it started and ends, and the payment reference and amount. Card payments are handled by our payment provider, LivePay; we never see or store your card number.
  • App check-ins: when an app is open and online it checks your plan with our server and records the time, the app name and your computer's name, so your plan works and we can help you if something goes wrong.

What we don't collect

Your photos, albums, projects and exports stay on your own computer. The apps do not upload them to us.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and unlock the plan you paid for, including offline use for the period you paid for.
  • To send you account emails you ask for, such as password reset codes.
  • To answer support requests.

We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Google user data

Smart Ease's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google sign-in data is used only to identify your Smart Ease account.

Where it is stored

Account and plan records are stored on our server and in our hosted database, protected by access controls. Sign-in sessions are stored only as one-way hashes.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete your account and its records at any time by emailing us. Deleting your account ends access to any paid plan.
