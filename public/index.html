Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use the applications completely offline?

Yes! Smart Ease Photobook Designer and Ease Select are native desktop applications built for high performance. Your images, project files, and designs remain completely private on your local storage.

How does license delivery work?

Immediately upon checkout confirmation, your unique license key (e.g. SE-2026-XXXX-PRO) is displayed on screen and emailed to your receipt address for instant activation.

What is the difference between single app and bundle?

The single application plan ($2.45/mo) unlocks either Photobook Studio or Ease Select. The Best Value Bundle ($3.00/mo) includes both applications under a single license, saving you 39% every month.

How does Lifetime Pro work?

Lifetime Pro is a one-time purchase of $200.00 with zero recurring subscription charges. It grants you permanent entitlement to all current and future software additions within the Ease Creative Suite.
